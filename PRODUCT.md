# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sales agents and managers at NK Herbal — a herbal supplement business. They use this daily on desktop, occasionally on mobile, to process orders, qualify leads, track customers, and monitor revenue. Primary task on any screen: find a record and act on it fast.

## Product Purpose

NK Herbal CRM: a sales operations dashboard for managing herbal product orders, leads, customers, WhatsApp conversations, and follow-ups. Success means a sales agent can open the dashboard, see what needs attention today, and take action in under 30 seconds.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, trustworthy. Not clinical — human enough to feel like a well-run shop, sharp enough to earn trust with real data.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered BI dashboards (too much happening, nowhere for the eye to land)
- Generic SaaS templates with gradient hero cards and decorative shimmer
- Overly playful consumer apps (wrong tone for a business ops tool)
- Heavy dark-on-dark UIs where text disappears into the background

## Design Principles

1. **Data first, chrome last.** Numbers and names should fill the screen; the UI shell should disappear.
2. **One thing per glance.** Each card, row, and section answers exactly one question. No multi-axis overloading.
3. **Earn familiarity.** Components should feel like tools the user already knows. No invented affordances.
4. **Consistent hierarchy.** Same heading weight, same label weight, same muted color — everywhere, without exception.
5. **Speed over ceremony.** Transitions ≤200 ms. No page-load sequences. The dashboard is a cockpit, not a showroom.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. All interactive states (hover, focus, active, disabled) visible without color alone.
